# Contributor covenant

Contributors are expected to create a respectful, inclusive, and learning-oriented environment.

- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Make assumptions visible.
- Treat failure as information while protecting customers and systems.
- Invite the people closest to the work into discovery and review.
- Credit source material and prior contributors.
- Escalate harassment, discrimination, or unsafe behavior to the maintainers.

Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate these expectations.
