# Definition of ready

A proposed Proof Pack is ready to build when:

- a single outcome and decision are stated;
- intended users and sponsor are known;
- scope, non-goals, and safety classification exist;
- discovery participants can represent the work;
- a service owner and repository owner are identified;
- likely evidence sources are accessible;
- source permissions and confidentiality are understood;
- no credential, customer data, or proprietary source is planned for the repository.

A shared capability is ready to build when it has a consumer, stable proposed boundary, named owner, and testable inputs and outputs.
