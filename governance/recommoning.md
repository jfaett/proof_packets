# ReCommoning policy

A contribution becomes shared only through a stewardship agreement.

## Admission criteria

- demonstrated reuse value;
- independent contract;
- tests and evidence;
- supported target statement;
- clear support boundary;
- maintenance owner;
- version and retirement policy.

## Decision

The shared owner and proposing consumers decide:

- **accept** — promote now;
- **accept with work** — promote after named gaps close;
- **incubate** — keep local while gathering another consumer;
- **decline** — duplication or maintenance cost exceeds reuse value.

Accepted contributions return through review, tests, and release. Consumers may extend locally, but improvements should be proposed back when they preserve the shared contract.
