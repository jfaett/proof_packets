# Labels and evidence

Use these labels consistently.

## ILLUSTRATIVE

An invented example intended to make the pattern concrete. It must never be presented as a result, benchmark, promise, or customer fact.

## CUSTOMER INPUT REQUIRED

A field that cannot be completed credibly without discovery or customer data.

## OBSERVED

A dated observation with a named source and collection method.

## HYPOTHESIS

A prediction with a planned test and decision rule.

## EXTERNAL EVIDENCE

A cited outside source. It can inform a hypothesis but cannot replace local proof.

## Evidence record minimum

- metric or control;
- source system;
- observation window;
- collection method;
- owner;
- raw or linked artifact;
- workflow/content version;
- redaction classification;
- result and acceptance decision.
