# Glossary

**Capability** — A reusable contract and implementation for a bounded action.

**Customer fact** — An observation or validated statement about the specific environment.

**Evidence checkpoint** — A point where workflow context, result, version, and verification are recorded.

**MBPM** — Metrics-Based Process Mapping; a detailed process map with actors, process time, lead or wait time, resources, and percent complete and accurate.

**Proof Pack** — The standard set of seller, discovery, process, technical, evidence, ownership, and expansion artifacts for one outcome.

**Qualified expansion** — An adjacent use case justified by local proof, sponsor intent, and capacity to own it.

**ReCommoning** — Returning an improved component to a shared commons through an explicit stewardship agreement.

**System of record** — The authoritative business or technical record, such as ITSM, CMDB, inventory, or evidence store.

**Use case** — An end-to-end service outcome, not merely a product feature or playbook.
