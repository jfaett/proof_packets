# SOP capture

## Purpose

Turn tribal knowledge into a shared description of the current work before deciding what to automate.

## Capture fields

For every step record:

- trigger and required input;
- actor and accountable owner;
- action in verb-object form;
- tool or system;
- decision or policy;
- expected output;
- common exceptions;
- evidence created;
- downstream consumer.

## Method

1. Observe or interview the people closest to the work.
2. Draft the happy path using their language.
3. Add approvals, waits, loops, and failure branches.
4. Walk a recent real example through the draft.
5. Mark disputed facts and missing evidence.
6. Obtain validation from a performer and owner.

## Quality test

The SOP is ready for decomposition when another knowledgeable person can follow the flow, identify every decision, and explain how success and failure are recognized. It is not required to be production-runbook detail.
