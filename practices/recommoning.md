# ReCommoning practice

ReCommoning is the intentional return of an improved component to a shared commons with an owner willing to steward it.

## Trigger

Use this practice when a capability has a second consumer, a meaningful improvement, or duplicated local variants.

## Conversation

1. What repeated need does the component address?
2. Which implementation is the proposed shared baseline?
3. Who consumes it and who is excluded?
4. What contract and tests must remain stable?
5. What maintenance cost will the commons accept?
6. Who becomes accountable owner?
7. What migration, version, and retirement plan is needed?

## Result

The outcome may be accept, accept with work, incubate, or decline. Declining is valid when the shared maintenance cost exceeds the demonstrated reuse value.
