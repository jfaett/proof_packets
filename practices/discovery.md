# Discovery

## Purpose

Align on an outcome, the people affected, the decision a proof must enable, and the uncertainties that could invalidate the use case.

## Participants

- outcome sponsor;
- service owner;
- people who perform the work;
- requestor or recipient;
- automation/platform owner;
- security, change, and evidence stakeholders.

## Prompts

1. What service outcome matters, and why now?
2. Who experiences the delay, risk, or toil?
3. What event starts the work and what proves acceptance?
4. Which tools already automate parts of it?
5. Where do people carry context or wait?
6. What must remain a human decision?
7. What happens when a check fails?
8. What evidence is assembled, where, and by whom?
9. Which measurable result would justify expansion?
10. Who owns the outcome after the demonstration?

## Outputs

- one outcome statement;
- actors and stakeholder map;
- scope and non-goals;
- fact/assumption/hypothesis register;
- customer decision and decision date;
- next mapping session.

## Facilitation notes

Start at the end: ask what success looks like and what evidence would change the sponsor's decision. Use Impact Mapping when multiple actors or behavior changes matter. Do not turn discovery into a feature interrogation.
